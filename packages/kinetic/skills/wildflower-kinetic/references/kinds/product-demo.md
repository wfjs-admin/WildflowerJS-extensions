# A product demo

A piece that shows a product working: an app's flow, a feature, a device.
Read with `../craft.md`.

## The intake, added

- Which task or feature is shown, and what should the viewer believe it
  does for them?
- Are there real screens, states and copy to use, or are they to be drawn?
- Who is the viewer: a customer, an investor, a team?

## For this kind

- **The interface is the hero**: large enough to read, with the rest of the
  frame quiet around it. A whole device in the frame shows where we are;
  each step then moves in close on the part of the screen it is about,
  until the interface's text reads at the caption's size or near it (the
  smallest label size in `style.md` at least), and moves back out between
  steps. A whole phone in a 16:9 frame leaves its text far too small to be
  the subject.
- **The device stays true**: a screen shown in a device is built as HTML
  inside it, so it stays sharp at any zoom; text inside it is the subject
  and keeps the app's own sizes (`../craft.md`, "Composition").
- **The focus follows the action**: the eye is led to where the tap, the
  change or the result happens, by a zoom, a highlight or a caption.
- **Real states, in order**: each step of the flow shown as the product shows
  it, with real or realistic copy, never placeholder text.
- **Orient first**: the product and what the viewer is about to see, before
  the first tap (`../craft.md`, "Motion and structure").
