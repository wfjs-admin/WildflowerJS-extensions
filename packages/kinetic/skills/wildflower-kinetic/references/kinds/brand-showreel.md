# A brand piece or a showreel

A piece about who someone is or what they have made: a studio's reel, a
brand film, a launch sting. Read with `../craft.md`.

## The intake, added

- What should the viewer feel about the brand at the end, in a word or two?
- What work or claims are shown, and in what order of importance?
- Is there a track, and is it to be cut to its beat?

## The dial and the direction

Set the expression dial before anything else (`../directions.md`, "The
expression dial"). A showreel, a sting or an opener is bold, a brand film
lively, unless the brief asks for calm. At lively or bold, the piece takes
its look and its motion from one direction (`../directions.md`, "Choosing a
direction"), in place of `../style.md`, and the plan names it with its
sentence. At calm, `../style.md` holds as for any piece.

`../style.md`'s defaults are made for a film that plays while people wait
for a meeting to start, and they make a reel dull: paper as the ground
throughout, a headline held 3 s or more, nothing moving between moves, no
bloom. A direction replaces them. `../craft.md`'s floors stay: the reading
floor for every word meant to be read, contrast, and no flashing.

## For this kind

- **The identity system leads**: the brand's colors, type and marks,
  applied consistently, with the direction's only where the brand is
  silent.
- **Each piece of work gets a moment that does it justice**: shown large,
  held long enough to see, with its name and one line.
- **Rhythm cut to the music** where there is a track: scene changes on the
  bar, accents on the beat. Without a track, choose a pulse anyway and land
  on it, on whole frames.
- **Transitions transform.** One scene becomes the next (a line becomes an
  eye, a shape opens into a room) instead of cutting, and each change can be
  said in a sentence. (A studio manifesto film has no hard cut in 110 s, measured.)
- **One palette unifies.** Scenes in different manners hold together through
  one palette applied throughout.
- **Close where you opened.** The closing motif echoes the opening one, and
  the end card holds 3 to 4 s.
- **Expression is a dial**, set once from the brief and kept for the whole
  piece.
- **Calm, then a burst.** Holds between short, fast changes: drift, snap,
  hold (`../directions.md`, "Laws for a lively or bold piece"). (Studio films and four showreels of 2023 to 2026, measured.)
- **No feature tour.** A reel shows a point of view, not every kind of move
  Kinetic can make: the direction's few moves, done well, throughout.
